## Full description (4000 max)
Metro Darts is a darts practice game and checkout trainer with a retro 80s neon look.

★ Checkout trainer — every finish from 2 to 170, with the standard checkout table
★ 501 darts against six characters, each with a dirty super power
★ Tournament mode — knockout bracket, legs and sets, and a prize for the champion
★ Nine-darter — the crowd builds, the place erupts, the stars come down
★ IN THE ZONE — chain perfect darts for fatter trebles and doubles
★ The Coach — rates every checkout and teaches the book route
★ Your throwing pace — record your own rhythm and every game plays to it
★ Score real darts games: 501 scorer, play a character on your own board

Built around one idea: rhythm wins. Swipe to pick up a dart, watch the ring run out to the edge of the board and back, and tap your target the moment it returns to the bull. Throw on the beat and your darts fly true; hesitate or rush and they drift.

CHECKOUT GAME
Random checkouts from 2 to 170. Hit them on the beat, in one visit, and gold bars stack up on your streak — ten become a big one. The checkouts get harder the longer you keep it going. String perfect darts together and you are IN THE ZONE: fatter trebles, doubles and bull for your next four darts.

501 VS CHARACTERS
Six opponents, each with their own game: the Viking takes his time, the Jockey never wobbles, the Lizzard is deadly on combination finishes, the Taylor scores heavily, the Bling only wants the bull and the double-double, and the Cockney scores for fun. Get them on the ropes and they fight dirty — every one has a super power: the Viking blocks the light, the Lizzard sets lizards loose on the board, the Jockey gets you drunk, the Bling blinds you with his jewellery, the Cockney turns the crowd on you, and the Taylor piles on the pressure until the trebles and doubles shrink. Legs and sets, bot strength scaled by difficulty, and a word from your opponent after every match — they are not gracious.

TOURNAMENT
Eight-slot knockout against the regulars, laid out as a bracket; your matches get longer and the opposition sharper every round. Lose and the draw plays out without you. Win it, and Double Trouble himself presents your prize — a turntable for the main menu, with two classic samples to scratch — then joins the opponents list. And someone you know rather well joins the next draw.

THE COACH
Every suggestion comes from the standard checkout table: the book route, why it is the route, and the alternative. Above 170 he keeps you off the bogey numbers and sets you up for a big finish. After every checkout he reviews what you threw against what you should have.

YOUR OWN PACE
Darts IRL → My throwing pace: stand at your real board, walk through a few turns at your natural speed, and save it. Every game then plays to your rhythm — or pick a steady, quickfire or lightning pro-style pace.

THE CROWD IS WATCHING
They sit up when the 180 is on, when you're on a double for the set, and when a nine-darter is on — and they go up the instant the winning double lands.

ALSO IN THE BOX
- Fast 501 solo for quick scoring practice
- 2-player 501 with legs, sets and a scorer's metronome, for a real board
- Checkout tool for real games: enter your score, get the route
- Synthesised 80s soundtrack, pub crowd, cheers, groans and a referee who calls your 180s
- Everything drawn in flat-shaded vector graphics with a CRT filter — no downloads, no ads, no accounts, works offline

Metro Darts collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone.
