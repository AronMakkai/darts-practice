Metro Darts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Metro Darts is a darts game for Android made by Aron Makkai. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: nothing.

Data we collect

None. Metro Darts does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no network access.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings and progress (difficulty, chosen pace, timing presets you record, best streak, selected opponent) in the app's private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Metro Darts requests no permissions. It asks the Android system which text-to-speech engine is installed so the in-game announcer can speak; it does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It contains mild comic banter and references to drinking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
